\section{Conclusion}

Within a deliberately narrow scope (Python CWE-22, Flask sources, intraprocedural analysis) the comparison supports four conclusions.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{A modest deterministic analysis is a strong refinement.} Constant folding, branch pruning, a loop fixpoint and a handful of sound containment patterns removed all the false warnings that were provably harmless, and never removed a real vulnerability.
\item \textbf{The value of an LLM is in what the analysis cannot prove,} namely the sufficiency of non-standard checks. That is also where it errs. Ordinary prompting judged nearby checks and reassuring names rather than the value actually consumed, and discarded up to a third of real vulnerabilities. Giving the model the extracted facts kept the gains and removed most of those errors. In our data, the representation mattered more than the model.
\item \textbf{A gate that verifies evidence structurally is necessary but not sufficient.} It guarantees that a suppression rests on the right value and a dominating check. It cannot tell whether the check confines the path, and both of its accepted mistakes were of exactly that kind. A post-hoc refinement that refuses containment-shaped but unsound guards fixed those two cases, but at the cost of correct suppressions on development data.
\item \textbf{The bottleneck is upstream.} Slices, dict stores, helpers in other functions and non-file sinks account for most missed vulnerabilities, and for four of six real-world targets. No triage stage can recover these.
\end{enumerate}

The practical lesson is that a triage stage should be measured by false-positive removal and true-positive retention \emph{together}. A deterministic arm should always be the control: here it matched the best LLM configuration on OWASP at zero risk.
