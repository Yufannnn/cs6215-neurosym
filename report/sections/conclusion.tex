\section{Conclusion}

Against the Semgrep baseline, evidence-guided triage raised precision on OWASP from 51.5\% to 62.5\% without losing a detected vulnerability, and on the diagnostic pairs it removed 15 of 19 false warnings while losing 2 of the 23 detected vulnerabilities. Within a deliberately narrow scope of Python CWE-22 warnings, Flask request sources and intraprocedural analysis, the frozen runs support four further conclusions about where that gain comes from.

First, a modest deterministic analysis is a strong control. Constant folding, branch pruning, a loop fixpoint and a few recognized containment patterns removed 36\% of false warnings on OWASP and 68\% on the pairs while keeping every vulnerability that the baseline detected. Two independent reviews still found programs on which this analysis wrongly proved a warning safe, so its suppressions are only as reliable as an implementation that is tested and not proven.

Second, on our diagnostic programs the LLM added correct suppressions only where the analysis could not prove that a non-standard check suffices, and that is also where it erred. Ordinary prompting trusted nearby checks and reassuring names over the value consumed at the sink and discarded up to a third of the detected vulnerabilities, whereas the evidence-guided configuration kept the removals and avoided most of those losses.

Third, a structural evidence gate did not pay off in our runs. The gate checks consistency with the extracted dependencies without checking that a guard confines the path, so it let through both wrong suppressions on the pairs, which rested on insufficient guards. All of its rejections hit correct suppressions, one from the primary model that cited a guard for a constant value and five from the smaller model that cited line numbers in place of evidence IDs.

Fourth, much is lost before triage begins. All 23 missed vulnerabilities on OWASP come from the handling of slices and dictionary stores in Semgrep CE, and in the real-world case study two of the four applicable targets were missed because the flow crosses functions or starts from an undeclared source, which no triage stage can recover.

A triage stage should be measured by false-positive removal and true-positive retention \emph{together}, and against a deterministic control. In this study that control matched the best LLM configuration on OWASP without a measured loss, and evidence given to an LLM helped mainly by keeping it from dismissing real vulnerabilities.
