\section{Conclusion}

Within a deliberately narrow scope of Python CWE-22 warnings, Flask request sources and intraprocedural analysis, the frozen runs support four conclusions.

First, a modest deterministic analysis is a strong control. Constant folding, branch pruning, a loop fixpoint and a few containment patterns removed 36\% of false warnings on OWASP and 68\% on the pairs while keeping every benchmark-labelled vulnerability. An independent review still found programs on which this analysis wrongly proved a warning safe, so its proofs hold only under the stated assumptions and only for the constructs it models.

Second, an LLM is most useful on what the analysis cannot prove, which is whether a non-standard check suffices, and that is also where it errs. Ordinary prompting trusted nearby checks and reassuring names over the value consumed at the sink and discarded up to a third of real vulnerabilities, whereas on our diagnostic suite the evidence-guided configuration kept the removals and avoided most of those losses.

Third, a structural evidence gate did not pay off in our runs. Because the gate checks dependency consistency without checking that a guard confines the path, it rejected no wrong suppression, rejected one and five correct ones on OWASP, and let through two mistakes that were both insufficient guards. A post-hoc variant that refuses containment-shaped but unsound guards fixed those two cases at the cost of correct suppressions on development data.

Fourth, much is lost before triage begins. All 23 missed vulnerabilities on OWASP come from the handling of slices and dictionary stores in Semgrep CE, and in the real-world case study two of the four applicable targets were missed because the flow crosses functions or starts from an undeclared source, which no triage stage can recover.

A triage stage should be measured by false-positive removal and true-positive retention \emph{together}, and against a deterministic control. In this study that control matched the best LLM configuration on OWASP without a measured loss, and evidence given to an LLM helped mainly by keeping it from dismissing real vulnerabilities.
