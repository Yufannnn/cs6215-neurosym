Taint analyzers report many path-traversal (CWE-22) warnings that turn out to be false alarms. An LLM can be asked to dismiss them, although every wrongly dismissed warning becomes a missed vulnerability. We build an evidence-guided LLM triage stage for Semgrep Community Edition and compare it on identical warnings with Semgrep alone, with plain LLM triage, with a deterministic control based on our own intraprocedural value analysis, and with a variant whose suppressions must pass a gate that checks the cited evidence against extracted facts. The held-out subjects are 134 cases from the OWASP Benchmark for Python and 24 diagnostic program pairs that we constructed, evaluated with \texttt{qwen3-coder-next} and \texttt{qwen3.5:9b} under a protocol frozen before the comparative test runs, together with a small case study of six real-world fix commits.

On OWASP, evidence-guided triage removed 12 of the 33 baseline false warnings and kept all 35 vulnerabilities that the baseline detected, which raised precision from 51.5\% to 62.5\% at an unchanged recall of 60.3\%. The deterministic control achieved the same result, so this benchmark shows no benefit of the LLM beyond it. On the diagnostic pairs, plain LLM triage discarded up to 35\% of the detected vulnerabilities, and structured evidence cut these losses from 7 to 2 at the same false-positive removal with the primary model and from 8 to 0 with the smaller one. The evidence gate prevented none of the observed wrongful suppressions. It rejected one correct suppression from the primary model, which had cited a guard for a value that was constant, and five from the smaller model, which had cited line numbers where evidence IDs were required. Two independent reviews found programs on which our extractor wrongly proved a warning safe, and after both corrections every held-out evidence record is unchanged, so every reported result stands. In the case study four targets fall within the task, and the baseline reported the documented defect in one of them, a fix-related flow in another, and nothing in the remaining two.
